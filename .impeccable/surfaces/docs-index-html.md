# Landing page — surface brief

Scope: the marketing page at `/` (`docs/index.html`), visitor mode **Persuade**.
The app at `/app/` is out of scope.

## Audience and action

Two audiences, one story: a writer at a desk and a teacher handing work to a
class. The page is judged by the teacher's decision to use Loomings with a
class tomorrow. The one action is opening the editor.

Proof on hand: the product itself, real screenshots of the current build, the
bundled Moby-Dick chapter and six lessons, the MIT source. No testimonials, no
numbers, no institution logos, and nothing may imply them.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The landing page is one transparency on an overhead projector — a
working sheet you read, switched on by the lamp — not a picture of a laptop,
and not the narrow printed column it is today.

OWN-WORLD: A dim room (#0E1112), a charcoal machine (#1C2124) with steel
labels (#97A1A3), and a lit acetate field (#EAF0E6) that carries the content.
Ink #14181A; one grease-pencil red #C0392B, reserved for the lamp switch and
the single state mark. Barlow Condensed for silkscreened labels and the
wordmark, JetBrains Mono for the source pane, Source Serif 4 for the rendered
pane and its display type, Caveat for one hand-written annotation.

STORY: Within one viewport a visitor knows this is a real Markdown editor in
the browser, that source and result are the same sheet, and that the only
thing to press is the lamp. Then: a document travels as a link, a lesson is
taught from it, the same page exists in four bindings, what it keeps, and the
switch again at the close.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A machine head across the top carries the wordmark and four
links. The lit plate fills most of the width: Markdown source in the left
half, the rendered chapter in the right, the same lines paired. The base
below holds three engraved claims, the red lamp switch as the single primary
action, and a clipped provenance ticket.

FORM: candidate 3 of the ordered list (overhead projector), behind the
classroom worksheet and the blackboard; seed:267890f0651bb0ce.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the
finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its
provenance.

## Memorable moment

Pointing at a line of Markdown lights its counterpart on the rendered side
(and the reverse): a red grease underline travels across the sheet, showing
the mechanism — the code becomes the page — rather than describing it.
