## Caption

icons are the longest single design decision in any app.

loomings went through about thirty drafts before landing on the italic didot L. earlier attempts tried a whale-fluke silhouette (too literal), a starburst (too generic), a flat coloured square (too forgettable), and a serif L that was too narrow at small sizes.

the final mark uses Apple's Icon Composer to apply the Tahoe glass effect — the squircle, the inner shadow, the subtle specular. the letter inside is italic Didot, the colour is the same amber that runs through the app.

four candidates above. the third is the one in your dock.
