## Caption

a markdown writing app, finally to one-point-zero.

loomings is what i wanted from a writing app and could not find — a blank page that loads instantly, real markdown in the editor, a focus mode that dims everything outside the current sentence, an outline you can jump to with ⌘P. no cloud, no account, no AI in the margin.

it is named for chapter 1 of moby-dick. the bundled example is that chapter.

macos · windows · linux · open source · MIT

install on apple silicon:
brew install --cask --no-quarantine tiagojct/loomings/loomings

other platforms and source at tiagojct.eu/loomings
