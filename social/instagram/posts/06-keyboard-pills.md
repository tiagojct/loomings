# 06 — Keyboard shortcuts

**Format:** carousel, 8 slides, 1080×1350
**Pillar:** the writing app
**Best slot:** week 2 Thursday

## Slides

Same template across the seven shortcut slides. Final slide is a CTA.

### Common template
- Background: navy `#0B1F2D`.
- Center large: the shortcut as keyboard pills.
  - Each modifier (⌘, ⇧, ⌥, F11) as a separate pill: 60 px height, amber outline, cream JetBrains Mono.
  - Between pills: small "+" in muted cream.
- Below the pills: thin amber rule, 80 px wide.
- Below the rule: the action name in italic Source Serif Display, 76 pt, cream.
- Below the action name: one-sentence explanation in regular Source Serif, 26 pt, muted cream.
- Bottom-right: `loomings.app` mark.

### Slides 1–7 (one shortcut each)

| # | Pills | Action | Explanation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ⌘ P | Outline | Fuzzy-jump to any heading. |
| 2 | ⌘ F | Find | Search the document, with regex if you want. |
| 3 | ⌘ ⇧ D | Focus | Dim everything outside the current sentence. |
| 4 | ⌘ ⇧ P | Preview | Toggle the rendered markdown. |
| 5 | ⌘ ⇧ W | Width | Cycle the column width — wide, normal, narrow. |
| 6 | ⌘ ⇧ T | Theme | Cycle the theme — system, light, dark. |
| 7 | ⌘ ⇧ G | Goal | Cycle a word goal — 0, 250, 500, 750, 1000, 2000, 5000. |

### Slide 8 (CTA)
- Background: parchment.
- Center: "Seven shortcuts. The rest stays out of the way." in italic Source Serif, 58 pt.
- Below: `tiagojct.eu/loomings` in mono.

## Caption

seven shortcuts is most of what loomings asks you to remember.

⌘P jumps to any heading. ⌘F finds. ⌘⇧D dims the page around your current sentence. ⌘⇧P shows the rendered preview. ⌘⇧W cycles column width. ⌘⇧T cycles the theme. ⌘⇧G sets a word goal.

everything else (open, save, bold, italic, find-replace) is the macOS / windows / linux convention. nothing weird to relearn.
