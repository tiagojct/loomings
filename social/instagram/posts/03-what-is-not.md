## Caption

most writing apps want a piece of you.

an account, a subscription, a margin assistant, a synced "workspace", a roadmap of features the team thinks you should want. loomings does not.

every "no" on these slides is a deliberate choice. some i may regret later. for now they are how the app stays small and gets out of the way.
