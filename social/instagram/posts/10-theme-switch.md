# 10 — Theme switch

**Format:** Reel, 1080×1920, 6 seconds
**Pillar:** the writing app
**Best slot:** week 5 Tuesday

## What to record

1. Loomings open in dark mode, the Moby-Dick example visible.
2. Press `⌘⇧T`. Watch the editor cycle: system → light → dark.
3. Hold each theme for ~1.5 seconds.

Crop to the editor window only. Burn captions.

## On-screen overlays

- 0:00: top of screen, JetBrains Mono 28 pt cream, `dark`.
- 0:02: top, `light`.
- 0:04: top, `system`.
- 0:05–0:06: centered amber pill, JetBrains Mono `⌘ ⇧ T`.

## Caption

dark, light, or follow the system. ⌘⇧T cycles.

both themes share the same palette — the parchment-ink of the light mode is the inversion of the cream-on-navy in the dark. amber stays amber. it makes the app feel like one thing even when you swap.

system-follow respects your macOS / windows / gnome appearance setting and listens for changes. flip your laptop into night mode at 8pm and the editor follows.
