## Image brief

Five horizontal color bands stacked vertically, each labelled with hex on the right edge in JetBrains Mono.

Top to bottom:

1. `#F1E7D2` — parchment    (light)
2. `#D4A882` — amber light  (accent)
3. `#BD8C68` — amber        (accent)
4. `#0D2F42` — pequod navy  (mid dark)
5. `#0B1F2D` — below deck   (deep dark)

Band height: 270 px each (5 × 270 = 1350).

On the leftmost ~40% of each band, the band's name in italic Source Serif:
- "parchment", "amber light", "amber", "navy", "below deck".
- Color of the label: contrast — ink on light bands, cream on dark bands.

On the rightmost ~25% of each band, the hex in JetBrains Mono with thin amber dot before it.

Top-left corner: small "Loomings" wordmark in italic Source Serif, 36 pt. Below it: "the pequod palette" in regular Source Serif, 22 pt, muted.

## Caption

the colour palette is borrowed from a chapter that opens with a man bored of land.

navy "below deck" for the dark theme, parchment for the light. amber as the only accent — the lantern light melville mentions further into the book. cream for the foreground in dark mode, ink for the foreground in light mode.

every surface in the app uses these tokens and only these. no hardcoded hex outside the palette definition. dark and light cycle with the system if you let them.
