## Caption

the app is named after a chapter.

chapter 1 of moby-dick is called *loomings*. it opens with the most famous sentence in american fiction and ends with ishmael deciding to go to sea because he is bored of land.

the bundled example file inside the app is that chapter. it loads with two clicks from the help menu and gives you something to scroll, edit, jump around with the outline palette. melville is in the public domain, so the text comes along for the ride.

if you have not read it in a while, *now* is a small and reasonable excuse.
