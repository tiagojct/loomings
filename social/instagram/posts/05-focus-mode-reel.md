# 05 — Focus mode (Reel)

**Format:** Reel, 1080×1920, 9 seconds
**Pillar:** the writing app
**Best slot:** week 2 Tuesday morning

## What to record

1. Open Loomings.
2. Open the bundled Moby-Dick chapter (`Help → Open Example`).
3. Click into the middle of a paragraph.
4. Press `⌘⇧D`.
5. Move the cursor slowly with arrow keys across two sentences. Watch the dim follow the active sentence.
6. Press `⌘⇧D` again to exit.

Record at 60 fps with `Cmd+Shift+5` → record selected portion. Crop to the editor window only — drop the menu bar and titlebar.

Trim the result to 9 seconds:
- 0–2 s: editor in normal mode.
- 2–6 s: focus mode active, cursor moving.
- 6–9 s: focus mode toggled off, return to normal.

## Caption

focus mode (⌘⇧D) dims everything outside the sentence under the cursor.

most "focus modes" hide the menu bar and call it a day. this one is structural — the editor knows where your sentences begin and end (paragraph-bounded), so as you write, the previous sentence fades, the next sentence fades, the one you are writing stays bright.

works in any markdown file. toggle it with ⌘⇧D, or click the option from the view menu.
