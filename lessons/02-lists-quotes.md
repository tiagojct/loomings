# Lists and quotations

## Bulleted lists

Start each line with a hyphen, an asterisk, or a plus sign, followed by a space:

- Milk
- Bread
- Coffee

Press Return at the end of a list item and Loomings starts the next one for you. Press Return twice to leave the list.

## Numbered lists

Start each line with a number, a full stop, and a space. The numbers do not have to be in order; the result renumbers them:

1. Preheat the oven.
1. Mix the dry ingredients.
1. Fold in the eggs.

## Nested lists

Indent a list item by two or more spaces to put it inside the one above:

- Fruit
  - Apples
  - Pears
- Vegetables
  - Carrots
  - Leeks

## Quotations

Start a line with `>` to quote someone. The quotation can run over several lines and hold its own paragraphs:

> Call me Ishmael. Some years ago, never mind how long precisely, having little or no money in my purse, I thought I would sail about a little and see the watery part of the world.
>
> Herman Melville, *Moby-Dick*, chapter 1.

## Try it

> **Exercise 1.** Write a shopping list of five items as a bulleted list.
>
> **Exercise 2.** Write the steps to make a cup of tea as a numbered list.
>
> **Exercise 3.** Under "Fruit", add a third kind of fruit, indented like the others.
>
> **Exercise 4.** Quote one sentence from a book or song you like, and attribute it on a second line inside the quotation.
