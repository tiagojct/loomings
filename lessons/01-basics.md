# Markdown basics

Markdown is plain text with a few marks that say how it should look. You are reading the *source* on the left and the *result* on the right. Edit the left side and watch the right side follow.

## Paragraphs

A paragraph is one or more lines of text. To start a new paragraph, leave an empty line.

A single line break inside a paragraph
does not start a new line. The two lines above join into one.

## Headings

A heading starts with one to six `#` signs and a space. One `#` is the title of the document, two is a section, three is a subsection.

### This is a third-level heading

Use headings in order: a section, then its subsections. Do not skip levels to make text bigger.

## Emphasis

Wrap a word in single asterisks for *emphasis* and in double asterisks for **strong emphasis**. You can combine them for ***both***. Underscores work the same way: _emphasis_ and __strong__.

Emphasis is for meaning, not decoration. Use it for a term you are defining, or for the one word that changes the sentence.
